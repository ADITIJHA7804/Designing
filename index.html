Phishing Protection Tips

Check the Sender

Verify the sender's email address. Phishers often use similar-looking domains or misspelled company names.

Hover Over Links

Before clicking, hover over links to see where they actually lead. Suspicious URLs are red flags.

Urgency Tactics

Be wary of emails creating false urgency like "Act now!" or "Account will be closed!"

Grammar & Spelling

Professional organizations rarely send emails with poor grammar or spelling mistakes.

Personal Information

Legitimate companies never ask for passwords, SSN, or credit card info via email.

Suspicious Attachments

Be cautious with unexpected attachments, especially .exe, .zip, or .scr files.
